Detailed Description

Error codes specific to schema validation.

These codes are surfaced through kcenon::common error_info via container_schema::validate_result(), so they MUST live inside common's reserved container_system band [-499, -400]. They occupy the schema-validation sub-band (-450 to -459), distinct from error_codes::'s validation sub-band (-410 to -419).

See also
kcenon::container::container_schema::validate_result
